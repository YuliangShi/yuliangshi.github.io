Yuliang Shi

About

I am a fifth-year PhD student in mathematics at the University of British Columbia, supervised by Omer Angel and Jonathan Hermon. I am also a visiting student at NYU Shanghai. I received my MASt in Mathematical Statistics from Cambridge and my BS in Honors Mathematics from NYU Shanghai. My research focuses on probability theory, particularly interacting particle systems, percolation, and random matching.

Email: yuliang@math.ubc.ca · Google Scholar
